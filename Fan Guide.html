Quick Fan Tips

  • Admission: All official Fan Festivals are 100% free to the public.
  • Transportation: Use public transit! Cities like Philly and NY offer free subway rides for fans on match days.
  • Safety: Be prepared for high decibel levels (up to 95dB) at major screenings.
